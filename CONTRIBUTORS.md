## Scientific Sources

Exactly three related-work sources are retained from the source notebook:

1. Harle, R. (2013). *A Survey of Indoor Inertial Positioning Systems for
   Pedestrians.* IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials, 15(3), 1281-1293.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/SURV.2012.121912.00075).
2. Woodman, O. J., and Harle, R. K. (2008). *Pedestrian Localisation for Indoor
   Environments.* UbiComp 2008, 114-123.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/1409635.1409651).
3. Faragher, R., and Harle, R. (2015). *Location Fingerprinting With Bluetooth
   Low Energy Beacons.* IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications,
   33(11), 2418-2428. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSAC.2015.2430281).

These explain related approaches; their reported performance is not a benchmark
for this coursework setup. The current implementation uses an RSSI likelihood,
not Faragher and Harle's fingerprinting dataset or model.
